Roadmap

A month of planned work, in four phases.

~1,000 issue-ready items, organized into four weekly phases. This is a backlog to draw from and convert into real issues at whatever pace makes sense — not a queue of 1,000 open issues, and not a promise that all 1,000 ship this month.

PhaseItemsFocus
Week 1250Deepen the audio engine (more modes, tempo/genre presets, auto-gain) and multi-bulb orchestration
Week 2250TLS/reverse proxy, PIN-gate hardening, the adversarial security-test phase, multi-user auth exploration
Week 3250Home Assistant/HomeKit/voice/Discord integrations, scenes & scheduling depth, mobile PWA
Week 4250Analytics, Bluetooth-readiness prep, GitHub Pages, agent onboarding, CI, release process, a real retro

How phases depend on each other

The security-test phase (Week 2) can't meaningfully start until TLS and PIN-gate hardening are in place — attacking a half-built defense wastes the exercise. Integrations (Week 3) lean on Week 2's auth story being settled. Real multi-bulb testing (Week 4) is gated on Week 1's orchestration work existing to test. Everything converges on Week 4's release + retro, deliberately last — the checkpoint that confirms the month's work is real, not just written down.

Two phases explicitly require infrastructure that doesn't exist yet at time of writing — the adversarial security test needs a real deployed, exposed instance; real multi-bulb testing needs a second physical bulb. Both are sequenced late in the month for exactly that reason.

Full itemized list for all four weeks, plus the dependency graph as a diagram: the complete roadmap/ directory.
